## The one insight (the never-seen-before)
Every "Allow?" button ever made conflates two different questions into one: "is it real?" and
"how far does it reach?" KEEP splits them into TWO channels on a single object:
- Channel 1 — TRUST-MATERIAL (is it real): solid milled metal (verified) / faceted glass
  (content-addressed) / frosted ghost (unsigned), rendered FROM the real ed25519 verdict.
- Channel 2 — BLAST-RADIUS HALO (how far it reaches): a cool→hot hazard band rendered FROM the
  runtime's real reversibility + scope + egress verdict, deliberately a different channel than
  the material, so reach reads separately from realness.
The gasp: a VERIFIED capsule can be visibly MORE dangerous (wide hot halo, whole Library,
one-way) than an UNSIGNED one (cold pinpoint, one object, reversible). For the first time you can
refuse the thing you were trained your whole life to trust. The contradiction is felt in the hand.
Un-fakeable: a clone must lie 3 times (metal on unsigned; a narrow halo on a wide act; or refuse
to sign its own liability).

## What it is, plainly
A calm dark command deck for the agentic life: where your AI agents' work LANDS as accountable
artifacts, and the single gate every action they propose must pass. Four things no environment
does together: (1) shows both truths (real + reach) on every capability; (2) one weighted seal in
front of every consequential act (heavier for irreversible, disclosure before the seal is
pressable); (3) records what agents did + whether they stayed in contract, in a chain you hold +
export, where the platform also signs its own accountability; (4) toggles to a plain familiar
desktop with one gesture.

## The pillars
- THE TWO-CHANNEL OBJECT (hero atom) — material + halo, both from real verdicts.
- THE WEIGHTED KEYSTONE SEAL — felt mass scales with the real reversibility/reach verdict; a hard
  consent-ordering invariant (never pressable until the manifest renders + dwell satisfied);
  non-haptic typed-confirm path satisfies the identical invariant (accessibility).
- THE DUAL RECEIPT — yours (exportable containment proof: "the agent stayed in its lane"; a
  deviation is a user-favorable signed "attempted X beyond contract, DENIED") + the platform's
  co-signed self-attestation ("the runtime executed correctly; ours to answer for"). Flips the
  legal fear into the moat. The single highest-leverage new build.
- THE REVIEW (daily-lovable, NO score) — a once-a-day glanceable digest of the 2-3 agent receipts
  most worth your eyes (biggest reach / first-of-type / budget-brushing); never suppresses; full
  chain always one gesture away.
- THE GRANT GARDEN — live scoped/expiring/revocable tokens as tended filament cords with
  AGGREGATE-REACH BUDGETS (egress cap, action-count cap, scope ceiling); the gate RE-SURFACES when
  cumulative reach crosses a threshold ("a secret read reversibly is leaked irreversibly"); one-
  gesture fail-closed revoke + a true ALL-STOP (real tokens go slack).
- BRING-YOUR-OWN-AI (MCP) — external AIs pass the SAME gate, entering from the side with a "via
  MCP" grain, receipts stamped under YOUR identity. Honest today: read/preview; act-over-MCP is
  the labeled, prioritized next dependency.
- USE-WITHOUT-OWNING — a dDRM Library asset glows keyed only during its gated open inside a visible
  decrypt sandbox, then re-seals. Honest framing: the gated window opened/closed under your
  authority (custody is operator-curated, surfaced in a trust-root readout), not trustless key
  destruction.
- THE GROUNDED GOAL COMPOSER (v1) — over real discovered affordances (NOT free-text NL = deferred).
- ERASURE-COMPATIBLE IMMUTABILITY (v1 invariant) — per-record encrypted payloads over a hash
  spine; GDPR erasure = crypto-shred the key, the chain's integrity over hashes survives.

## The toggle (refraction, not reboot)
One gesture slides glass across the whole screen (~320ms); positions preserved, same files under.
GENERIC = flat daylight, ordinary windows, consent layer recedes to a quiet bolt-on. KEEP = the
charcoal void returns, every object picks up both real channels. The mid-wipe frame (half boring
desktop / half signed material with live halos) is the category's 10-second pitch and cannot be
faked (rendered from real state). Leaving the gated world widens reach, so the flip is itself a
gated, receipted act. The toggle caps the downside: worst case KEEP degrades to a familiar OS with
a real consent layer.
