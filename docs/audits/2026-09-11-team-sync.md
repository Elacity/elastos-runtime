## Process lessons and next meeting decisions

Repeated coordination, broad reviews and large task histories consumed substantial effort. The loop caught real defects, but accepted journeys advanced more slowly than implementation. Account-wide allowance readings cannot isolate this project's cost.

Development-loop skill 0.4.1 now requires one useful installed result, an early evidence checkpoint, and a route change when effort outpaces acceptance. The owner and reviewer check dependencies first, revise affected goals after each result and reuse unchanged evidence. Lower effort per accepted behavior still needs measurement.
