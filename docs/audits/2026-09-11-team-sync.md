## Process lessons and next meeting decisions

The development loop caught real defects, but repeated coordination, broad reviews and large task histories consumed substantial effort. Accepted user journeys advanced more slowly than implementation. The available account-wide allowance readings cannot isolate this project's exact cost.

Version 0.4.1 of the development-loop skill now requires one useful installed result, an early evidence checkpoint, and a route change when effort outpaces acceptance. The owner and reviewer check assumptions and dependencies before execution. After a result, they revise only the affected next goals and reuse unchanged evidence. The improvement still to demonstrate is lower effort per accepted user behavior.

For the team sync, agree the next installed acceptance slice and its owner/reviewer, then resolve these items:

4. Schedule the two-role Browser campaign and agree when its owned resources are available.
