# ElastOS weekly report, 10 - 16 October 2026

Seven signed test releases went out on canary this week, from 0.8.0-alpha.15 to alpha.21 (alpha.21 at 06:25 on 16 October). Chat, the phone Home and the Mac Browser all reached `develop`, and the alpha.19 device checks passed with no blocker. 0.8.0 was due on 16 October. On the evening of 15 October I held it, because one Home's Community froze for more than 55 minutes after another Home on its network restarted ([#93](https://github.com/Elacity/elastos-runtime/issues/93#issuecomment-6207122080)). alpha.21 carries the recovery fix. A security audit then found one more blocker, a stall when a Community takes in new messages ([#401](https://github.com/Elacity/elastos-runtime/issues/401)). Its fix is planned for alpha.22. OMP expects it ready to sign 11:00 to 11:30 on 16 October, with 0.8.0 in the early afternoon (an estimate, not yet on GitHub). The latest tagged release is still v0.7.1. Last week: [2 - 9 October](https://github.com/Elacity/elastos-runtime/blob/docs/weekly-2026-10-09/docs/audits/2026-10-09-team-sync.md).

## CI, CD and builds

From Saturday to Friday morning, Actions ran 142 times: CI 126 (67 passed, 4 failed, 48 cancelled, 6 queued, 1 running), Release package 15 (10 passed, 3 failed, 2 cancelled) and one Carrier NAT run. Most cancellations are intermediate train runs. By actor: me 102, cursor[bot] 18, Sasha 15, Irzhy 7. That is fewer than last week's 292 because each train is checked once.

cargo audit now runs in CI ([#351](https://github.com/Elacity/elastos-runtime/pull/351)). A full `develop` run takes 40 to 67 minutes and the Mac job is the slowest part (34 to 61 minutes). A release package takes about 75 to 80 minutes, almost all of it the Mac build. `develop` pushes do not cancel older runs, and docs-only changes still run the full set (ci-speed-2026-10-15.md). One known flake ("installed Runtime exited before health") needed a rerun on alpha.21. A cloud agent looked into it and found no evidence ([#386](https://github.com/Elacity/elastos-runtime/pull/386), closed).
