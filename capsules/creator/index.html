How this works

Protection. Your file is encrypted before it is published. Decryption keys are split across independent dKMS nodes — decentralized operators, not a single company vault. No one node can unlock your content alone; recovery requires a quorum (2-of-3) using post-quantum cryptography. You sign every step from your own wallet; this app never holds your keys.

Ownership & market. Minting deploys a smart contract on Base that represents your asset and tokenizes access rights (editions). Buyers receive rights — not a raw file copy. Once the mint confirms, enable trading so those rights become transferable capital others can buy, hold, and resell on your terms.
