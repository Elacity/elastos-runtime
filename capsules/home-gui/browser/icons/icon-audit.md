# Final icon audit

## First-principles material grammar

The family uses a frontal, simplified source drawing and lets a small set of material
effects create richness: separated layers, translucent color, frosting, rounded edge
highlights, subtle chromatic shadows, and one consistent top-left light. Sharp corners,
thin lines, complex perspective, and baked-in photorealistic effects were removed or
avoided. Every metaphor is designed to survive at 32 px.

## Final gates

- System and Controls have different silhouettes and concepts.
- Only the dedicated `elastos` asset uses the ElastOS logo.
- Marketplace is an unmarked bag.
- Capsules contain no orange arrow or directional seam.
- Fixed personality colors remain independent of the live OS accent.
- No Apple, Microsoft, Google, Nintendo, SF Symbols, Fluent, Material, or Nucleo
  artwork was copied, traced, or embedded.
