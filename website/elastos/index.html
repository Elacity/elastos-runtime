Inside Home

Files, contacts
and settings.

Home brings your apps together. Use Documents to write, Library to find your files, and People to manage your profile and contacts.

01Write and organise+

Start a document, return to a draft, or find something you saved. Documents and Library work with the files in your Home.

02Create your profile+

Choose the name people see when they connect with you. People keeps your profile and contacts together.

03Look after your account+

Save a Recovery Kit during setup and keep it somewhere safe. Open System Settings from the ElastOS menu to manage your account, appearance and services.
